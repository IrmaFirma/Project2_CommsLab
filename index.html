The Brew and the Beak

Wizard Ethan wants to marry a dark witch Aziza. All he has to do is befriend a legendary owl and survive one very polite cup of coffee.

How to read

  • Click the picture to move the story on or start an animation. Your cursor turns into a hand when you can.
  • Nothing happening? You probably haven't clicked yet.
  • Autoscroll is on. The page scrolls along with the story, so you don't need to scroll yourself.
  • Choose an ending when the buttons appear.
